BRANDING (OPTIONAL)
===================

Put a logo here as:

    logo.png

A transparent PNG works best. It appears small in the outro and in the
bottom-right of the thumbnail.

Turn it off, or point at a different file, in config.yaml:

    theme:
      logo_enabled: true
      logo_file: "logo.png"
      logo_height: 0.10      # fraction of the frame height
      logo_opacity: 0.9

To add a channel name to the outro:

    theme:
      channel_name: "Your Channel"
      channel_tagline: "Quran with Urdu translation"

Leave them empty and the outro stays clean.
